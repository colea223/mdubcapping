College Football Ratings

Dub Gamma's live Net Rating for every FBS team, plus SP+'s Offense/Defense/Special Teams split, schedule-strength ranks, and a rest-of-season win projection.

Read these as tiers, not exact positions -- especially early in the season, the top of the order can still move a lot week to week.

Glossary
Net Rating
Dub Gamma's own live power rating (the same number used for every spread/win-probability pick on this site) -- higher is better, and the scale isn't points-per-game or wins, just a relative power scale.
Off / Def / ST
CFBD's SP+ offensive, defensive, and special-teams ratings. Higher is better for Off and ST; LOWER is better for Def (it's scaled like points allowed).
SOS Rank
Strength Of Schedule rank -- #1 is the toughest schedule played so far, based on the average rating of every FBS opponent faced.
SOR Rank
Strength Of Record rank -- #1 means the best resume: how many results a team has earned above what an exactly-average team would have gotten against that exact schedule.
Projection
Current wins plus Dub Gamma's own win probability summed across every remaining game left on the schedule -- a projected final win total, not a playoff/seeding projection.
